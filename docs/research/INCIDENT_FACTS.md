# Adoshi incident: attributed facts and unresolved details

Research date: 8 November 2026. Times below are IST (UTC+05:30). These are reported accounts, not a forensic reconstruction.

| Topic | Reported information | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Around 17:00 on 3 February 2026; rollover near Adoshi tunnel on the Mumbai-bound carriageway | SRC-002, SRC-003 |
| Cargo | Approximately 21,000 kg of propylene | SRC-002 |
| Damage | Multiple damaged valves; specialists described changing liquid/vapour leakage | SRC-002 |
| Transfer timing | TOI places transfer initiation around 16:30 on 4 February; Indian Express gives around 18:00 | SRC-002, SRC-003 |
| Reopening | Around 02:00 on 5 February, with backlog clearing afterwards | SRC-003 |
| Total escaped | Approximately three tonnes, attributed to experts/officials | SRC-001 |
| Conditions | Responders credited favourable winds and described ongoing gas monitoring | SRC-001 |

## Interpretation limits

- Preserve the transfer-start disagreement. Do not invent minute-by-minute telemetry.
- The overall response/closure duration is not the duration of a constant release or a single transfer.
- Total cargo and reported loss do not establish opening size, peak release rate or thermodynamic state.
- A reported detector observation at a distance is not a usable validation record without its value, units, location, time and instrument details.
- No field plume trajectory, concentration dataset or verified accident coordinates have been acquired.
- Exact destination, receiving-tanker count and later recovery phases should be verified before adding detail to the demo.
